---
name: grill-me
description: Ask 2–4 clarifying questions to uncover constraints, success criteria, and context before tackling ambiguous requests.
---

Ask targeted clarifying questions when a request is vague, under-scoped, or lacks critical context. Pick questions that would actually change your approach:

- Scope/success criteria: "What does done look like?"
- Constraints: deadlines, backwards compatibility, performance budgets?
- Root cause: is this the real problem or a symptom?
- Context: what's been tried, what's in-flight elsewhere?

**Example**: User says "Make the tests faster"
- Are we aiming for 50% faster or just any improvement?
- Can we parallelize, or are tests interdependent?
- Have you profiled to find bottlenecks?
- Is this blocking development, or nice-to-have?

Ask then wait for answers before proceeding.
